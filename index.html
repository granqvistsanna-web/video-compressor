Video Compressor

Drop in a video loop, get a lean MP4 out. Resized, with or without sound, under a size you cap it at. Runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Drop videos here or click to choose · MP4, MOV, WebM
Max widthLong edge. Never upscales
SizeA ceiling in MB, or a fixed quality
SoundBackground loops autoplay muted anyway

Output is H.264 MP4 with fast start, so it begins playing before it has finished loading — the format every browser and Framer's Video component play.

Encoding uses your device's video hardware through WebCodecs: Chrome, Edge, Safari 17+ and recent Firefox. Change a setting and drop the file again to redo it.
